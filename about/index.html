About

The Submarine Vet, and the tools he builds.

Silent Service Trading is the software platform of The Submarine Vet. Command Center is its first product.

The story

From reading instruments to building them.

Submarine service teaches a particular kind of discipline: read your instruments instead of guessing, follow procedure the same way every time, and keep the boat inside its limits — through long, quiet stretches and through the moments that suddenly matter.

Trading futures turned out to reward the same habits. Running several funded accounts at once, the hard part wasn't finding setups. It was keeping track: which account was near its limit, what had copied where, what was still open.

Command Center started as the console he built for his own trading in NinjaTrader. It became Silent Service Trading's first product.

Instruments over instinct.

What we believe

  • You make the decisions. Command Center copies and protects the trades you place. Playbooks alert; they never enter for you.
