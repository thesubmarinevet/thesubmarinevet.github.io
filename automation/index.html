SilentService – NYOpen

Trade the New York open breakout, the same way every day.

NYOpen marks the overnight range and trades the first clean break of it after the New York open — one trade a day, with a fixed stop and target, flat before the close.

  • Range: 3:00–9:30 AM ET
  • Entry: first break above or below the range, 9:30–11:00 AM ET
  • Fixed dollar stop and target per contract
  • One trade a day; flat by 3:55 PM ET
  • Turn each weekday on or off
  • ES and NQ (and their micros)

How to read these numbers.

  • Profits are before commissions, exchange fees and slippage. At a few dollars per round trip, about 200 trades cost several hundred dollars more than shown, and real fills — especially fast breakout fills — can be worse than the backtest's.
  • The strategy's settings were chosen with this same 2026 data in view. Results on data the settings never saw would likely be weaker.
  • The strategy is still being adjusted before release. These results will be replaced when the release version is tested.
