What happens next

Four steps to your first NYOpen session.

  1. Watch your inboxYour NYOpen license key and the strategy file arrive by email at the address you used at checkout. Check spam too.
  2. InstallClose NinjaTrader 8. Copy the NYOpen file into Documents\NinjaTrader 8\bin\Custom\Strategies, start NinjaTrader, open the NinjaScript Editor (New → NinjaScript Editor) and press F5 to compile. If you received a .zip instead, use Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On.
  3. Enter your keyAdd SilentService - NYOpen to a chart or the Strategies tab. Paste your key into License Key, the first setting. SST Lifetime owners can leave it empty — the SST key on that PC is used.
  4. Start on SimPick a simulation account and ES or NQ (or MES / MNQ), keep 1 contract, and enable it. The bottom-left of the chart shows your license status. Go live when you trust it.

Without a valid license, NYOpen turns itself off when you enable it and the Log tab says why. If a license lapses while it runs, it takes no new trades — stops, targets and the 3:55 PM flatten keep working.

Check your prop firm's rules on automated trading before running it on a funded account.
