Pricing questions

What does Lifetime include?

One payment, never billed again. You get every future upgrade of SST, plus every automated strategy and every playbook Silent Service Trading releases, for as long as they're offered. Only the first 25 lifetime seats are sold.

How long do launch prices last?

Launch prices are offered through December 31, 2026. If you subscribe before then, your monthly or annual price stays the same for as long as your subscription stays active. If it lapses, a new subscription is at the price offered at that time.

What happens when I cancel?

Monthly and annual plans run to the end of the period you've paid for. After that, SST won't start new sessions. Nothing about your NinjaTrader accounts or positions is changed.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Email us and we'll help you move between monthly, annual and lifetime.

Is sales tax included?

Tax is calculated at checkout based on where you are. Checkout and billing are handled securely by Stripe.
