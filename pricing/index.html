Pricing questions

What does Lifetime include?

One payment, never billed again. You get every future upgrade of Command Center, plus every bot (automated strategy) and every playbook Silent Service Trading releases, for as long as they're offered. Only the first 25 lifetime seats are sold.

What happens when I cancel?

Monthly and annual plans run to the end of the period you've paid for. After that, Command Center won't start new sessions. Nothing about your NinjaTrader accounts or positions is changed.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Email us and we'll help you move between monthly, annual and lifetime.

Is sales tax included?

Tax is calculated at checkout based on where you are. Checkout and billing are handled securely by Stripe.
