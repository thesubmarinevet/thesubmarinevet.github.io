Walkthrough

How a session runs in Command Center.

Enter, manage and copy trades from one console.

Place the trade once. Command Center copies it to your follower accounts, each with its own size and limits, and keeps every bracket on the instrument it was placed on.

  • Buy, sell, reverse, close and trim ¼ or ½ in one click
  • Use your own NinjaTrader ATM templates
  • Copy minis as micros, with contract caps per account
  • FLATTEN ALL ACCOUNTS when you need everything closed now

Watch every account, and let the rules enforce themselves.

Enter each prop account's limits once. When an account reaches its daily loss or drawdown limit, Command Center locks it and flattens it — even if you're not watching.

  • Day P&L and balance for every account, sortable
  • Drawdown, daily limits and consistency per account
  • Connection and copier status always in view

Plan the session, then review it.

Market Read puts the day's key levels on the chart. After the close, the journal has already recorded every trade, so review starts with the facts.

  • Prior day, session and opening-range levels, VWAP and POC
  • Playbooks that alert and mark the chart — never place orders
  • Daily results, per-account views and an equity curve
