Silent Service Command Center puts a trade copier, your prop-firm rules and a one-click kill switch in a single window inside NinjaTrader. It runs on your own machine. You still make the trades — it keeps every account inside its limits while you do.

The problem

Prop accounts don't blow up on the chart. They blow up in the admin.

A follower that copied one contract too many. A daily loss limit nobody was watching on account four. A position left open on the instrument you switched away from. When you trade five accounts at once, the risk isn't the setup — it's keeping track.

Copy with limits

Every follower has its own size, contract cap and daily loss / profit lock.

Rules that enforce themselves

Enter each firm's limits once. Hit one and that account locks and flattens.

One button out

FLATTEN ALL ACCOUNTS: copier off, every position closed, every order cancelled.

What's inside

Built for traders running several funded accounts.

Trade copier

Trade one account; the rest follow, each on its own terms.

  • Size per follower: multiplier and max contracts.
  • Minis to micros: copy NQ as MNQ, ES as MES and more, with a max-micros cap.
  • Daily loss / take-profit lock per follower.
  • STEP-DOWN: one follower takes each trade until it reaches its target or loss limit, then the next account takes over — with the exit orders working on the follower.
  • Copier ON / OFF right in the console header.

Prop-firm rules

Enter each account's rules once. The Command Center watches them every second.

  • Per account: stage, drawdown, daily limits, consistency %, max micros.
  • A limit hit locks the account and flattens it.
  • Locks lift at the next trading day.
  • Your automated strategies can read each account's max micros and size down to fit.
  • Sim accounts hidden with one toggle.

Kill switch

For the moment you need everything closed, now.

  • FLATTEN ALL ACCOUNTS — one click, no confirm dialog.
  • Turns the copier off first, so followers don't fight the flatten.
  • Closes every position and cancels every working order, on every account.
  • Logs what each account was holding.

Trade Console

Order entry built so you don't misclick under pressure.

  • Buy / sell, stop loss and target, reverse, close, cancel.
  • Trim ¼ / ½ in one click.
  • Buttons never move. Messages appear below them, never above.
  • Switch instruments freely — orders keep working on the one they were placed on.

Accounts & journal

Every account's day at a glance, and a record of every trade.

  • Day P&L and balance per account, sortable.
  • Open P&L on the chart's position line.
  • Trades logged automatically; the strategy that placed a trade is noted on it.
  • Exclude "just for fun" accounts from your stats.

Chart & levels

The levels most intraday traders draw by hand, drawn for you.

  • Prior day high / low, Asia / London / NY session ranges, premarket, opening range.
  • VWAP, POC from tick data, daily pivots, fair value gaps.
  • See which automated strategies are running, and on what.

Runs on your machine

Your accounts, your machine, your kill switch.

The Command Center runs inside NinjaTrader on your own PC or VPS. Your broker and prop-firm connections stay where they are. There's no cloud server holding your logins and trading your accounts for you.

What it does

  • Copies the trades you place — or your own NinjaTrader strategies place — to your other accounts.
  • Enforces the limits you set for each account.
  • Gives you one place to see and control every account.

What it doesn't do

  • No signals, no "done for you" trading. You decide every trade.
  • No promises of profit. Futures can lose money fast — that's why it exists.
  • It doesn't replace your firm's own rules. Read them; some firms restrict copiers.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it trade for me?

No. You place the trades — by hand, or with your own NinjaTrader strategies. The Command Center copies them to your other accounts and enforces the limits you set. Playbooks, when they arrive, only alert and mark the chart.

Is it allowed by my prop firm?

Rules differ by firm, and they change. Some firms allow copiers across your own accounts, some restrict them, and some limit automated trading. Check your firm's current rules before you copy to its accounts. You're responsible for following them.

Does my computer need to stay on?

Yes. It runs inside NinjaTrader, so NinjaTrader has to be open and connected while you trade. Many traders use a Windows VPS for this.

Which brokers and accounts does it work with?

Any account you can trade in NinjaTrader 8 — including the prop-firm accounts connected there and NinjaTrader's simulation accounts.

Can I try it without risking money?

Yes, and you should. Set it up on NinjaTrader simulation accounts first, test your copier group and rules, then move to live accounts.

What does Lifetime include?

One payment, never billed again. You get every future upgrade of the Command Center, plus every bot (automated strategy) and every playbook Silent Service Trading releases, for as long as they're offered. Only the first 25 lifetime seats are sold at this price.

What happens if I cancel?

Monthly and annual plans run to the end of the period you paid for. After that the Command Center won't start new sessions. Nothing about your NinjaTrader accounts or their positions is changed.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Email us and we'll help you move between monthly, annual and Lifetime.

Do you see my account data?

No. Your accounts, balances and positions stay inside NinjaTrader on your machine. The license check sends only your license key and a machine ID.
